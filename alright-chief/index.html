Alright Chief

First-pass prototype · v0.1

A transition app for new dads who want to feel like themselves while becoming the father their family needs.

Everything is clickable. Data lives in your browser only.

Language interface

Conversational copy is generated live by Claude when a key is connected; otherwise the scripted fallback voice is used. Status: scripted fallback

Key is stored in this browser only and sent directly to the Claude API.

Avatar pipeline (Midjourney)

The buddy's art direction is a Midjourney prompt built from the dad's own answers. The in-app SVG creature is the live stand-in.
