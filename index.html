01 / British Army

Aligned stakeholders to deliver an award-winning website, on time and on budget

The DecisionAlign every stakeholder to three guiding principles before designing anything.

The British Army website serves millions of people a year, from serving soldiers’ families to a new generation discovering military history. Brand, Comms and Military Leadership all wanted different things, so I aligned them around three principles (Unity, Modernity, Excitement) and measured every decision against them. I also proposed an archive-led experience for Soldier Magazine. The site received the Award of Excellence from the International Communicator Awards.

02 / RIBA

Reduced development and design costs by 50%

The DecisionOne design system for every RIBA product, built to outlast any agency.

I led the translation of a new visual identity into a single design system for riba.org, the RIBA Journal and the wider product portfolio. 03 / Frasers Group

A 400% increase in sign-ups in the first week of launch

The DecisionMove account registration into the app’s onboarding flow.

One app scaled across 15 brands, from Sports Direct to Flannels. I led the UX and brand decisions for the rebuilt m-commerce experience, and moving registration into onboarding drove the uplift. One adaptable design system saved significant time and resource, with many brands rolled out without agency support.

04 / Echodyne

A site that scaled with the team, with 13 locales launched in year 1 to support expansion into new markets

The DecisionMove to Webflow instead of rebranding.

A defence-tech market leader that still looked like a startup. A rebrand was logical, but not the answer: the content team couldn’t move at the speed the business needed on their CMS. I made the case for Webflow, a lighter, more adaptable platform than Umbraco, enabling a three-month delivery in time for a key sales period.

Questions

01 What kind of brands do you work with?

Established brands heading into their next phase of growth. You’ll usually have a solid visual identity and a working website in place already, run by an agency or your own marketing team. That’s where my work makes the biggest difference.

02 Do you work with startups?

Yes, when the timing’s right. I love helping startups reach their goals, and the right moment to bring me in is when you’re approaching, or have just closed, Series A funding.

03 We’re not sure which service we need.

Start with a Decision Audit. It’s a bespoke engagement that helps leaders work out their next move. That might confirm what you were already planning, redirect resource to a different project altogether, or save you time and money by improving what you already have.

04 Won’t a Decision Audit just give us another strategy deck?

No. It ends in a decision: what to do, what not to do, and what it will realistically cost and return. You get a clear recommendation you can act on and defend to your board, not a document to file.

05 What is your pricing?

Hidden pricing is one of the most frustrating parts of finding a partner, so here’s mine. You’ll always know the cost before we start.

  • Decision Audit From £6,000. A fixed fee, usually delivered in under 30 days.
  • Fractional Design Leadership From £4,000 a month. 4–6 days a month, with a three-month minimum.
  • Capability Building Typically from £30,000 over 3+ months. A fixed fee for each phase, agreed upfront.

All prices exclude VAT. Every engagement is scoped to your situation, so if you’re not sure what fits, start a conversation and I’ll give you my honest advice. Sometimes the right answer is a smaller piece of work first.

06 Do you offer ongoing support or retainers?

Not open-ended ones. You’ll work with me for exactly as long as you need me, and no longer. Fractional Design Leadership is the one ongoing arrangement, and even then the aim is to leave your team with the tools, knowledge and confidence to manage without me.

07 We’re not based in the UK. Can we still work together?

Absolutely. I’m based in the UK, but most of my work is delivered remotely. As long as you’re happy to work in English and collaborate online, we’re good to go.

08 What happens when we get in touch?

Email me with what you need help with, or book a 30-minute call and we’ll say hello properly. Before we speak, I’ll ask for a few key details, so neither of us wastes any time.
